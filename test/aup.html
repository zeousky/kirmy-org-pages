Acceptable Use Policy

This page collects the rules for how you use Core, how accounts work, the API, what isn’t allowed, and what happens if you break a rule.

Acceptable use Account security and responsibility Unauthorized access and security API use and automated requests Prohibited malicious activity Enforcement

Acceptable use

  1. Only use Core for lawful things, and follow Discord’s own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  2. Use each service for what it’s for, and stay inside the plan and limits on your account.
  3. Don’t use Core to harass, scam, impersonate, or harm anyone, and don’t build or run anything that does.

Account security and responsibility

  1. Your Core account is your Discord account. You’re responsible for keeping it secure.
  2. If it happens on your account, we treat it as you, unless you told us the account was compromised before it happened.
  3. Keep API keys, tokens, and other credentials private. Don’t put them in public code, screenshots, or messages.
  4. Tell the Core team quickly if you think your account or a key has been stolen or leaked.

Unauthorized access and security

  1. Don’t open an account, dashboard, page, endpoint, or data you weren’t given access to.
  2. Don’t try to bypass, turn off, or probe sign-in, sessions, roles, or any other security control.
  3. Don’t try to get permissions you weren’t given. That includes raising your own access, faking requests or IDs, and changing data on the way in or out.
  4. Don’t exploit a vulnerability. If you find one, tell support and don’t use it or share it.

API use and automated requests

  1. You can send automated requests only where a service says that’s allowed, and only inside the limits it publishes.
  2. Don’t flood, scrape, stress-test, or otherwise overload Core, and don’t use extra accounts or tools to get around a limit.
  3. Don’t resell, share, or publish access to Core, or to keys, unless your plan specifically allows it.

Prohibited malicious activity

  1. Don’t upload, run, or share malware, phishing pages, or anything meant to damage or take over a system or account.
  2. Don’t use Core, including a VPS, to attack, scan, or disrupt other systems, or to host anything illegal.
  3. Don’t use a VPN or proxy to hide abuse. Core may block VPN and proxy connections to protect the service.

Enforcement

  1. If you break these rules, Core can act without warning when it needs to. That can mean signing you out, limiting or banning your account, blocking an IP, removing content or servers, or ending your access.
  2. We log what we do. When we can, we tell the person why they were restricted.
  3. Trying to get around a restriction, for example with another account, can make it longer or permanent.

Last updated 10 October 2026. Questions about these rules go to Core support. Availability and liability are in the Terms of Service.
