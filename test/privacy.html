Privacy notice

Core uses a few cookies so you can stay signed in, and it looks at connection details to keep the service safe. Dock is what links your Roblox account when you pay with Robux.

If you submit a purchase claim, we store your Discord ID, the Roblox account Dock verified, the product, whether it was a gift, and when the claim happened. That record lives in a separate payments database.

If you donate in USD, Stripe’s checkout stores your Discord ID, what you chose, the amount, the currency, Stripe’s payment IDs, and the time we confirmed the payment. We never get your card number.

Don’t send us a Roblox password, cookie, or security code. We don’t use ad cookies or cookies that track you across other sites.

IP address

We check your IP for VPNs, proxies, rate limits, and abuse. Day to day we keep a keyed hash, not the raw address. If an admin bans an IP on purpose, that address is saved with the moderation record and the audit log.

Cookies

Your sign-in cookie stays in the browser and holds the session details. The server checks its signature on each request. We don’t also save that cookie as a session in the database. There’s a short-lived cookie for Discord sign-in, and a random visitor cookie used only to count anonymous visitors in memory.

You can clear cookies whenever you want. That signs you out. This page is only explaining that. Closing the notice on the Core service doesn’t change your cookie settings.
