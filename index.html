25 minutes · Serves 2

Smoky tomato chickpeas

Spoon them over toast so the bread catches the sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 × 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 × 400 g tin chickpeas, drained
  • 1 tsp cider vinegar
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Warm the oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Stir the garlic for 30 seconds, then stir in the paprika.
  2. Add the tomatoes right away. Stir in the chickpeas and a pinch of salt. Simmer uncovered for 15–18 minutes, until the sauce clings to the chickpeas.
  3. Crush a few chickpeas against the side of the pan. Stir in the vinegar and season to taste.

30 minutes · Serves 2–3

Coconut red lentils

The lentils cook down to a soft, spoonable stew.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 150 g split red lentils, rinsed
  • 200 ml coconut milk
  • 350 ml water
  • Salt and ½ lemon

Method

  1. Soften the onion in the oil over medium heat for 6 minutes. Stir in the curry powder for 30 seconds.
  2. Add the lentils, coconut milk, water and ½ tsp salt. Bring to a simmer, then cook gently, uncovered, for about 20 minutes. Stir occasionally and add a splash of water if it gets too thick before the lentils are soft.
  3. Squeeze in lemon to taste. Serve with flatbread or rice if you have some.

15 minutes · Serves 2

White beans on toast

Press about half the beans into the oil. The others can stay whole.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • ½ tsp dried rosemary, crushed
  • 1 × 400 g tin cannellini beans, drained
  • 4 tbsp water
  • ½ lemon
  • 4 slices of bread
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Warm the oil and garlic over medium-low heat until the garlic smells sweet, about 1 minute. Add the rosemary, beans and water.
  2. Simmer for 6–8 minutes. Mash roughly with a fork, squeeze in lemon and season.
  3. Toast the bread and spread the warm beans over it.

25 minutes · Serves 2

Tomato and butter pasta

A little pasta water helps the butter and tomatoes come together.

Ingredients

  • 200 g dried short pasta
  • 30 g butter
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 × 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil. In a frying pan, melt two-thirds of the butter and cook the onion until soft, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add tomatoes, oregano and a pinch of salt. Simmer uncovered for 15 minutes while the pasta cooks.
  3. Before draining, save half a mug of pasta water. Toss the pasta with the sauce and remaining butter. Loosen with a splash of pasta water and season.

35 minutes · Serves 2

Crisp rice with peas

A wide pan gives the rice space to form a crust.

Ingredients

  • 150 g long-grain white rice
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 150 g frozen peas
  • 1½ tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp rice or cider vinegar
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Cook the rice as directed on the packet. Fluff it and let steam escape while you cook the peas.
  2. Fry the onion in half the oil in a wide skillet for 5 minutes. Stir in the frozen peas and cook until hot all the way through. Tip into a bowl.
  3. Heat the remaining oil over medium-high heat. Press the rice into the pan and leave it alone for 3 minutes. Turn it in broad patches and cook for another 2–3 minutes, until crisp in places. Fold in the peas, soy sauce, vinegar and pepper.

25 minutes · Serves 2

Mustard lentils and potatoes

Dress the potatoes while they’re hot so they take up the mustard and vinegar.

Ingredients

  • 450 g potatoes
  • 1 × 400 g tin green lentils, drained
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 small shallot, finely chopped
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Cut the potatoes into 2 cm pieces. Cover with salted water, bring to a boil and simmer until fork-tender, about 12–15 minutes.
  2. While they cook, whisk the oil, vinegar, mustard, shallot and a pinch of salt in a large bowl.
  3. In the last minute, add the lentils to the potato pot to warm through. Drain well and toss with the dressing. Finish with pepper.
